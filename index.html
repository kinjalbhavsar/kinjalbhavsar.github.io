01

Where it began

I grew up in Nadiad, a small city in Gujarat, India — the kind of place where curiosity about how things worked usually meant taking them apart and hoping to get them back together.

That curiosity turned into a career that kept pulling me across the map: from Gujarat to a chip-design town in Taiwan, to grad school in North Carolina, and finally to the hills of the Bay Area, where I've been ever since.

Three continents, a lot of late-night debugging, and one constant — I still get a kick out of figuring out how things work.

02

What I build now

These days I'm a software engineer at Google, working on Android XR — specifically Jetpack XR SceneCore, the developer SDK people use to build spatial, 3D apps.

Before that I spent years on ARCore, and before that on camera systems, Linux kernels, and the kind of embedded firmware that quietly runs underneath everything. Somewhere along the way, code I worked on ended up running on 2+ billion devices — still a strange and wonderful thing to type out.
